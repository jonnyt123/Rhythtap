Bring your own charts

Import .tap files.

RhythmTap can import Phoenix-style .tap charts, attach local audio, save chart offsets, and keep custom content stored in your browser.

Supported local audio includes MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, AAC, and FLAC. Custom audio stays on the player's device rather than being uploaded to RhythmTap's backend.

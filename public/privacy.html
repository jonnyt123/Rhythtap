Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

This policy explains how RhythmTap handles information when you use the game, create an account, compete online, or purchase RhythmTap Pro.

Information we process

  • Account information, such as your email address, user ID, display name, and authentication records.
  • Gameplay information, including scores, rankings, progression, multiplayer results, settings, and entitlement status.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device/browser data, timestamps, error details, and network information.
  • Subscription records, including Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, billing status, plan interval, and renewal or cancellation state.

Payments

Stripe processes payments and hosts Checkout and subscription-management pages. RhythmTap does not receive or store complete card numbers. Stripe handles payment information under its own privacy policy.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and gameplay, validate scores, operate multiplayer features, grant paid entitlements, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, communicate service information, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

RhythmTap uses service providers—including Supabase for authentication and application data, Stripe for billing, and hosting/deployment providers—to operate the service. They process information only for the services they provide and under their own applicable terms.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. RhythmTap uses access controls and server-side authorization to protect account, billing, and gameplay data, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may update available account and billing information through RhythmTap or Stripe’s Customer Portal. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to records that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, or transaction purposes.

Children

RhythmTap is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information may contact RhythmTap to request review or deletion.

Changes

This policy may be updated as RhythmTap changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, open a private-safe support request without including passwords, payment-card details, or other secrets at RhythmTap Support.
